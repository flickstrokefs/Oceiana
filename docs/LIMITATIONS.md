# LIMITATIONS.md — Scientific Limitations, Assumptions & Uncertainty Bounds

**Platform:** Oceiana / ARIEL 2.0  
**Target Basin:** Bay of Bengal ($5^\circ\text{–}23^\circ\text{N}, 80^\circ\text{–}100^\circ\text{E}$)

---

## 1. Spatial Resolution & Sampling Sparsity

1. **Argo Profiling Float Density:**
   - Autonomous profiling floats drift passively with deep ocean currents ($1000\text{ dbar}$ parking depth).
   - In the Bay of Bengal, operational float density averages approximately **1 float per $300\text{ km} \times 300\text{ km}$**.
   - Sub-mesoscale and coastal shelf processes ($<50\text{ km}$ spatial scale), such as river plume fronts and internal tides, are not resolved by autonomous floats alone.
   - Coastal shallow regions ($<200\text{ m}$ depth) cannot be sampled by standard Argo floats due to grounding risk.

2. **Autonomous Gliders:**
   - High spatial-resolution glider missions provide dense transects along specific survey corridors, but their geographic coverage is episodic and localized rather than basin-wide.

---

## 2. Temporal Sampling & Aliasing

1. **10-Day Profile Cadence:**
   - Standard Argo floats surface every 10 days (or 5 days for specialized rapid-profiling missions).
   - Diurnal solar skin heating cycles ($<24\text{ hours}$) and high-frequency internal gravity waves ($<12\text{ hours}$) are aliased.
   - Reference depth for mixed layer calculations is fixed at $10.0\text{ dbar}$ specifically to bypass diurnal skin heating noise.

2. **Cyclone Passage Cold Wakes:**
   - A tropical cyclone transits a marine area in 12–36 hours, inducing intense vertical mixing and SST cooling ($1\text{–}3^\circ\text{C}$).
   - Unless an Argo float happens to cycle immediately beneath or within the eye-wall radius during transit, the instantaneous pre-to-post storm oceanic enthalpy drawdown must be reconstructed using satellite microwave SST and operational reanalysis.

---

## 3. Remote Sensing & Optical Limitations

1. **Monsoon Cloud Cover:**
   - The Bay of Bengal experiences dense, persistent tropospheric cloud cover during the Southwest Monsoon (June–September) and Northeast Monsoon (October–December).
   - Infrared Sea Surface Temperature (SST) sensors (e.g. MODIS, VIIRS) and optical ocean-color sensors (chlorophyll-a) suffer from significant data dropouts due to cloud masking.
   - Microwave SST (e.g. AMSR2) penetrates non-precipitating clouds but has coarser spatial resolution ($\approx 25\text{ km}$) and cannot measure within $50\text{ km}$ of coastal land.

2. **Skin vs Bulk Temperature:**
   - Satellite radiometers detect infrared emission from the ocean skin layer ($\approx 10\text{–}20\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ depth).
   - In-situ sensors measure bulk thermodynamic temperature ($>0.5\text{ m}$ depth). A standard skin-bulk correction of $\approx -0.17^\circ\text{C}$ applies under moderate wind conditions.

---

## 4. Operational Numerical Models vs In-Situ Ground Truth

1. **Spatial Representation Mismatch:**
   - A float CTD sensor takes point soundings with millimeter-scale response time.
   - Numerical models (e.g. CMEMS $1/12^\circ$ operational physics) represent volume-averaged grid cells ($\approx 9\text{ km} \times 9\text{ km}$).
   - Fine vertical temperature micro-structure and sharp pycnocline step-features are smoothed by model diffusion.

2. **Thermocline Wave Displacement Errors:**
   - Model-observation validation demonstrates that temperature RMSE peaks sharply in the $50\text{–}100\text{ m}$ depth band ($\text{RMSE} \approx 0.92^\circ\text{C}$).
   - This error is primarily driven by vertical phase offsets of internal waves: a vertical displacement of just $15\text{ m}$ in a $0.15^\circ\text{C/m}$ thermocline produces an apparent temperature error of $>2.2^\circ\text{C}$.

## 5. Non-Negotiable Scientific Guardrails & Policy Disclaimers

1. **No Digital Twin or Data Assimilation Claims:**
   - Oceiana is an **ocean observation exploration, diagnostic calculation, and report generation platform**.
   - It does NOT run operational primitive-equation forecast models, and does NOT perform 4D-Var or ensemble Kalman filter data assimilation. Operational forecasts presented are direct outputs of external operational agencies (CMEMS).

2. **No Unsubstantiated Fishery Claims:**
   - Oceiana provides oceanographic habitat context (thermocline and mixed layer depth bands where pelagic biomass aggregates).
   - It does NOT claim "50% fuel savings", does NOT guarantee commercial catch tonnage, and does NOT make unverified species-specific target assertions.

3. **Statutory Navigation & Safety Notice:**
   - Automated reports generated by this software are intended for scientific research, academic education, and decision-support exploration.
   - They do NOT replace official maritime weather bulletins, cyclone alerts, or Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) broadcasts issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD) or the Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services (INCOIS).
