## 4. Two-Round Evaluation Strategy

### Round 1: Poster & Stall Display Defense (Interactive)
* **Convention Reality:** In Round 1, judges walk around stalls with clipboards. They spend **3 to 5 minutes** per project. If a project requires a 10-minute setup or shows static slides, judges move on.
* **OCEIANA Stall Setup:**
  * **Center:** Laptop running OCEIANA connected to a large external secondary monitor (or iPad) in full-screen 3D mode.
  * **Background:** High-resolution A0 scientific poster featuring:
    1. 4D Architecture flowchart.
    2. Side-by-side comparison graph: ROMS Numerical Model vs. Argo In-Situ Sounding.
    3. TCHP Cyclone heat map of the Bay of Bengal.
  * **The 30-Second Hook:** As the judge approaches, rotate the 3D globe to the Bay of Bengal, drag the depth slider to 100 meters, and say:
    > *"Professors, what you are seeing is not a pre-rendered animation. This is a live, browser-native 3D digital twin of the Bay of Bengal, calculating TEOS-10 seawater thermodynamics across 16,000 in-situ Argo float soundings in real time."*

### Round 2: Oral Podium Presentation (Strict 7 Min + 3 Min Q&A)
* **Strict Rule:** Exceeding 7 minutes results in judges cutting the microphone, leaving findings unpresented.
* **Exact Time Allocation:**
  * **0:00 – 1:00 (The Crisis):** 20-year stagnation of desktop ocean tools (ODV/Ferret); disconnect between forecast models and in-situ instruments during cyclones.
  * **1:00 – 2:30 (The Architecture & Scientific Engine):** TEOS-10 equations, 10-dbar pressure gridding, CF-NetCDF parser, three-tier provenance tracking.
  * **2:30 – 4:30 (Live Platform Demonstration):** Surface view $\to$ Subsurface dive $\to$ Vertical exaggeration slider $\to$ Click live Argo float $\to$ Model vs. Sensor comparison modal.
  * **4:30 – 6:00 (Societal Impact & Operational Validation):** Cyclone heat potential in the Bay of Bengal, fuel savings for artisanal fishers via PFZ advisories.
  * **6:00 – 7:00 (Cost, Commercialization & Conclusion):** 100% open-source stack, elimination of ₹5L GIS licenses, roadmap for INCOIS deployment.

## 5. Potential Pitfalls in Round 2 (Podium Defense)

1. **Attempting a Live Demo via Venue Wi-Fi:** University convention Wi-Fi is notoriously unstable. If the team attempts to fetch live data from external APIs and the internet drops, the demo freezes and the round is lost.
   * **Mandatory Fix:** Run the backend locally on `http://127.0.0.1:8000` with local NetCDF files and disk cache.
2. **Dividing Presentation Between Multiple Speakers:** AIU rules allow only **one lead presenter** on the podium. Attempting a tag-team presentation will incur an immediate penalty.
3. **Getting Bogged Down in React/CSS Code:** Judges are university deans and scientists. They do not care about Tailwind or Vite plugins; they care about physical oceanography, scientific methodology, and real-world disaster reduction.
